# KALGARD Systems - Applications

## Primary Applications

### Defense and Security UAVs

MerlinSight Astrel supports UAV operations that need resilient positioning when GNSS performance becomes unreliable or hostile. MerlinSight Arrow, in development, is listed publicly as targeting only.

### Critical Infrastructure Monitoring

The system is relevant for inspection and monitoring missions where operators need continuity of positioning around strategic infrastructure. MerlinSight Sillage, in development, is listed publicly as mapping only.

### GNSS-Degraded Operations

KALGARD addresses environments where satellite positioning can be degraded, unavailable, jammed, or spoofed.

## Public Advantages

1. GNSS-independent absolute positioning when satellite signals cannot be trusted
2. Continuous position with periodic absolute correction against preloaded georeferenced reference data
3. Under 5 m mean 2D error measured in real flight (MerlinSight Astrel)
4. GPS-like NMEA/MAVLink output to the flight controller, with no autopilot modification
5. INRIA incubation and active field validation
